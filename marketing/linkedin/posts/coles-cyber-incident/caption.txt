This week, one of Australia's major supermarket chains said it is investigating a cyber security incident and has called in the Australian Cyber Security Centre.

It says it has found no evidence that customer, team or supplier data was compromised, and describes this as "a time of increasing cyber incidents in Australia and around the world".

Companies of that size put serious money and whole teams into protecting their customers' data, and incidents still happen. So the question for every practice is a simple one: what is protecting yours? Ten or twenty years of client files, precedents and know-how is the most valuable thing you own. Where does it live today, and who can reach it?
